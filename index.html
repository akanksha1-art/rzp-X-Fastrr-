Where Razorpay is credible — do not fight head-on

  • Offers and coupons: an AI coupon engine with 12+ coupon types, auto-apply best coupon, and a PDP affordability widget with personalised offers.
  • Payment breadth: 100+ payment methods including BNPL and EMI, with Snapmint available on request.
  • RTO protection: COD blocking for past RTO behaviour, and reimbursement on failed COD deliveries on its product page.
  • Delivery timeline at checkout: available through shipping profiles, so frame the gap as accuracy, product-page placement and pre-filled pincode, not "no delivery date at all".
Razorpay claims above and in CP·08 to CP·16 are taken from Razorpay's public Magic Checkout documentation and product page, reviewed Nov 2026, and describe what is stated there, not what may exist behind a sales conversation. Talk track — lead with the data, not the feature list

  1. Razorpay Magic Checkout is a strong payment-gateway-led checkout — but it's built to concentrate volume on Razorpay's own gateway. Fastrr is PG-agnostic by design.
  2. Razorpay's prefill and risk signals come from its payment network; Fastrr's come from Shiprocket's shipping network — a different, delivery-grounded data source.
  3. Open with 0% MDR blended pricing and pay-after-delivery for margin-conscious, prepaid-hesitant merchants — these are the two hardest points for a PG-led checkout to match.
  4. Walk the shopper's journey: delivery date on the product page, upsell in the cart, Quick Pay and SSO at checkout, then routing and Pay After Delivery at payment. Razorpay can match single features. It cannot match the path, because the path runs on delivery data.
